            ℹ️ What can convert to what
          

          
Images ↔ PNG/JPEG/WebP/AVIF/GIF (GIF keeps 256 colors — screenshots and simple graphics convert well, photos lose depth) · Images/TXT/CSV/Markdown/DOCX/EPUB → PDF · PDF ↔ text/Markdown/HTML and PDF → PNG/JPG pages · XLSX ↔ CSV/JSON · JSON ↔ YAML/XML/CSV · ZIP/TAR/GZIP · fonts TTF/WOFF/WOFF2 · MP3/OGG/M4A/FLAC → WAV or MP3 · videos (MP4/WebM/MOV) → GIF. Proprietary formats (DWG, PAGES, RAW photos, HEIC) can't be decoded locally, so they're honestly not offered.
