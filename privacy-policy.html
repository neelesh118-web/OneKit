OneKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

OneKit ("the extension") is a local browser toolbox. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the extension touches, where that data lives, and what you can do about it.

The short version

Everything OneKit does happens on your device, inside your browser. The extension has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Data is never uploaded, sold, shared, or even seen by the developer. You can delete it at any time with one click.

What data does OneKit use?

Because OneKit is a toolbox of 139 tools, it touches the following categories — all processed locally on your device:

  • Website content. Tools like full-text history search, page summaries, highlights and keyword analysis read the text of the page you are currently viewing, so they can work on that page. This happens only while the page is open and only in your browser.
  • Web history. The full-text history search and screen-time tools build a local index of pages you have visited. This index is stored only in your browser's extension storage and is never transmitted.
  • Personal communications. The AI Chat Vault can save conversations from AI chat sites (such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini) so you can search them later. This feature is off by default — it only stores a conversation if you turn the feature on and choose to save it.
  • Authentication information. The password vault and 2FA (TOTP) authenticator store credentials you enter yourself. These records are encrypted on your device before storage.

Where is the data stored?

All data is stored locally using your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage). Secrets — passwords, 2FA codes and encrypted notes — are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from a passphrase you choose. No decryption keys ever leave your device.

What is NOT collected

  • No account, sign-up or email address is required or collected.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, telemetry or crash reports.
  • No mouse movement, keystroke or click monitoring.
  • No location, health, financial or payment data.
  • No data is sold or shared with any third party — there is nothing to share.

Network requests

The extension makes no background network requests. A few tools make a network request only when you explicitly run them — for example, the API tester, bulk link checker and scam-site radar — and those requests go to the addresses you choose, not to the developer.

Your control

  • Delete: Settings → Your data, or the Privacy sweep tool, lets you clear everything stored by OneKit.
  • Backup & export: All your data can be exported or backed up locally, and restored on any device you choose.
  • Uninstall: Removing the extension deletes its data from your browser.

Children

OneKit is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date. Because the extension processes no data off-device, changes will rarely affect what is described above.
